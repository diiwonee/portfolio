Final Reflection

Looking Back

Most Interesting

Ridiculous Tariffs

Discovering that Kazakhstan can't negotiate trade deals independently — it needs permission from Russia, Belarus, Armenia, and Kyrgyzstan — was genuinely surprising. Tariffs are rarely just economics.

Most Challenging

Linear Optimization

Moving from intuition to formal notation — objective functions, decision variables, constraint sets — required a different kind of thinking. Getting the Pizza Optimizer to actually work was deeply satisfying.

Most Valuable

Comparative Advantage

The Ricardian model answered a question that seemed obvious but wasn't: why would a superpower ever trade with a less productive country? The math proves it's always worth it.

Skills Developed

Models & Critical Thinking

Mathematical modeling, reading economic data critically, scenario analysis, and the ability to spot when a government's stated reason for a policy doesn't quite match its actual effect.
