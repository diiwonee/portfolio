Looking Back
Most Interesting

Decision Trees

Mapping out consequences five levels deep makes explicit how much uncertainty compounds with each decision. One branch changes everything downstream.

Most Challenging

LP Notation

Moving from intuition to formal mathematical notation — objective functions, decision variable sets, constraint inequalities — required a completely different mental mode.

Most Valuable

Optimisation Mindset

Every real decision has constraints. Every constraint shapes what's possible. Formalising that structure — making the invisible scaffolding visible — is the core skill this course built.

Skills Developed

Modelling & Critical Thinking

Mathematical modelling, reading LP problems, building decision trees, network graph thinking, and using AI tools to generate and verify solutions.
